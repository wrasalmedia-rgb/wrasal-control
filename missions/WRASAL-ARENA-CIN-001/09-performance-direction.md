# 09 — Actor Performance Directions

Dual-use document: (a) direction language for live performers at look-dev/plates; (b) **performance verbs** translated into generative prompts (micro-expression grammar). Safety and compliance notes included.

---

## 9.1 CH-01 — Young Archer (SHOT-A)

**Objective:** hold the draw without betraying fear. **Tactic:** stillness as armor.
- Beat 1 (0.0–1.0 s): settle — shoulders drop 1 cm, bow arm locks out, gaze acquires target off-left.
- Beat 2 (1.0–2.0 s): aim squint — left eye closes, right eye dry-wide (no blink), brow knit low (concentration, not anger).
- Beat 3 (2.0–3.0 s): breath hold — chest frozen at half-inhale; lips sealed, jaw soft; nostril flare minimal.
- Beat 4 (3.0–4.0 s): tremor & resolve — 1–2 mm tremor in draw elbow; chin lifts 1° (resolve), hold.
- **Body:** weight 60/40 back foot; draw elbow in arrow line (`08` §8.4); hood still.
- **Prompt verbs:** "child archer holding full draw, eyes narrowed in aim, breath held, slight tremor in drawing arm, jaw set, silent concentration".
- **Compliance:** minor on set → licensed chaperone, limited hours, certified archery coordinator, prop bow with safe draw weight; synthetic generation keeps context defensive/sportive, no gore, no victimization.

## 9.2 CH-02 — Mentor (SHOT-A, torso)

- **Objective:** be the wall she stands behind. **Tactic:** zero motion.
- Torso still except 0.5° weight sway over 4 s; hands out of frame; breathing shallow (robe fold motion ≤1 mm).
- **Prompt verbs:** "out-of-focus adult torso in charcoal robe at frame edge, completely still, protective presence".

## 9.3 CH-05 — Village Survivor (SHOT-B)

- **Objective:** escape low and fast without standing. **Tactic:** crawl with purpose.
- Beat 1 (0–2 s): contralateral crawl-push, knees and palms, head down between shoulders.
- Beat 2 (2–3 s): hand slip on sand → hip drops 10 cm, instant catch (no flail).
- Beat 3 (3–4.5 s): recover to low crawl, speed +20%.
- Beat 4 (4.5–6 s): glance up 25° at ANOM-01 while moving (eyes first, head second), then head down.
- **Body:** robe hem drag; dust kick per knee strike; breathing audible panic-suppressed.
- **Prompt verbs:** "person in white robe crawling fast and low through burning village street, stumbles on loose sand, recovers without standing, glances up in terror while moving".
- **Safety (live):** fire bar, burn suit under robe for stunt performer, padded crawl lane, SFX fire only.

## 9.4 CH-03 — Woman Warrior (SHOT-C)

- **Objective:** turn retreat into charge with one word ("가자!" = "Let's go!"). **Tactic:** scream as command, not panic.
- Beat 1 (0–0.8 s): inhale against wind — chest expands, head turns 5° more into wind, eyes wet-squint.
- Beat 2 (0.8–2.0 s): onset — jaw drops wide (2-finger height), tongue low, teeth visible top+bottom; eyes OPEN and focused 90°; neck cords engage.
- Beat 3 (2.0–3.0 s): sustain — volume plateau, slight head shake on accent, hair whips 270°.
- Beat 4 (3.0–3.5 s): cut — mouth snaps to glare, chest heaves once, gaze locked.
- **Anatomy notes for gen:** correct dental arch, no extra teeth, jaw hinge natural, no neck twist beyond 90° profile.
- **Prompt verbs:** "warrior woman screaming a command in profile, jaw wide, eyes open and focused, hair streaming in wind, fierce not fearful".
- **Safety (live):** vocal coach, limited takes, eye protection for dust wind (blower rig screened).

## 9.5 Rider (SHOT-D) — CH-04 / CH-03-rider

- **Objective:** own the storm. **Tactic:** balance as declaration.
- Beat 1 (0–1.5 s): horse rises (2-beat); rider's seat slides back, legs forward-guard, one hand reins, other free or rein-held (G1 lock).
- Beat 2 (1.5–3.0 s): hold the rear — micro-adjusts: hips pulse 2×, shoulders level, head up toward storm.
- Beat 3 (3.0–5.0 s): cape full extension; rider's silhouette line unbroken (no limb flail).
- **Prompt verbs:** "rider holding a rearing horse in silhouette, seat deep, shoulders level, cape fully extended in wind, monumental stillness within motion".
- **Safety (live):** trained horse + fallaway footing, stunt rider, airbag vest under costume; **or** CG/synthetic horse per G1 decision (silhouette makes synthetic low-risk).

## 9.6 Performance-translation doctrine for generative prompts

1. Use **verb chains with amplitudes** ("settles, then squints, then holds breath") — models track ordered micro-actions better than adjectives.
2. One emotional register per shot; forbid mixed affects in negatives (`12`).
3. Specify gaze vector explicitly ("eyes fixed off-frame left") — eyeline drift is a top drift failure (QC-015).
4. Specify what must NOT move (hood, mentor torso, horizon) — stillness anchors temporal stability scoring (rubric C6).
