## 1. Camera System Decision

| Decision | PROPOSED Selection | Alternative | Rationale |
|----------|-------------------|-------------|-----------|
| **Primary Body** | ARRI Alexa Mini LF (or Sony Venice 2) — LF, 4K+ | FX9, RED Komodo (smaller) | LF sensor preserves shallow-depth control with scope crop; 4K+ required for SH020 thread legibility |
| **B-Camera** | Identical LF (second body) for simultaneous B-cam coverage on dialogue scenes (SH015–017, SH030–031) | Single body, reposition | 2 bodies allow continuity-matched coverage in church corridor without relight |
| **Lens Family** | Cooke Panchro Classics (LF) — warm, human, gentle falloff | Zeiss Supreme Prime (sharper) | Panchro warmth supports tragicomedy→horror falloff; sharp family would feel clinical |
| **ND/ISO** | Base ISO 800, ND .3–1.2 as needed to hold T2.8–T8 split | — | Maintain aperture control for macro depth vs portrait separation |
| **Color Pipeline** | Arri LogC3 → Rec.709 dailies; P3 D65 grade | Raw only | Dailies must read 1980s warm vs 2026 neutral difference |
| **Shutter** | 180° (24fps) — no off-angle unless SH020 movement test requires 90° to reduce motion blur | — | Standard cinematic motion blur; thread movement must read naturally |
| **Anamorphic vs Sphere** | Spherical 2.39 crop (not anamorphic glass) — PROPOSED | Anamorphics (e.g., Cooke Anamorphic) | Spherical crop preserves macro edge sharpness for thread; anamorphic squeeze complicates SH020 focus stability — DP may elect spherical for control |

## 2. Coverage Strategy

### 2.1 Coverage Principles

- **Witness Stance:** Camera does not search, chase, or conjure — it observes what community observes. No whip pans to reveal scares. No handheld intensity as substitute for blocking.
- **Forensic Close-up as Primary Evidence:** SH013–014, SH018–020 use macro ECU as *evidence imaging* — camera is microscope.
- **Communal Wide as Social Evidence:** SH002 master, SH007 mid-wide — camera is congregation witness.
- **Threshold as Geography:** Bedroom door appears in 4 shots (SH024, SH028, SH029, SH030) — camera holds same axis so audience learns threshold's meaning.

## 4. Sensor & Framing Mathematics

| Spec | Value | Calculation |
|------|-------|-------------|
| **Capture** | LF Open Gate 4.5K (4448×3096) | Provides 2.39 extract with headroom for reframing |
| **Delivery** | 2.39:1 extract (e.g., 3840×1606 @ 4K DCI crop) | All framing composes for 2.39 (see framing notes per shot) |
| **Macro magnification SH013–020** | 1:1 to 1:2 on full-frame → 12cm hem fills scope width ~70% | At 60mm 1:1: subject 24mm wide captured → 12cm hem = 5× sensor width — actual: use 1:3 (0.33×) to fit hem; test: 60mm at 0.33× + 2.39 crop = hem fills frame as spec |
| **Focus stability SH020** | T5.6–T8 depth required to hold 1.5cm travel | At 60mm macro T8.0, DoF ~4mm at 1:2 — may need focus bracketing; test or use probe lens alternative (Laowa 24mm Probe for deep DoF macro — PROPOSED backup) |

## 5. Movement Doctrine

| Movement Type | Allowed? | Shots Permitted | Prohibited Use |
|---------------|----------|-----------------|----------------|
| **Locked sticks** | Default | All forensic + masters | — |
| **Subtle push/drift (≤2 inches)** | Limited | SH007, SH031 (attention shift) | No dramatic crash zooms |
| **Handheld drift** | Limited | SH012 (comic energy) optionally, SH025 B-cam | No shaky horror — handheld would fabricate fear camera didn't earn |
| **Walk with subject** | Limited | SH025 Reader entrance (1.5s walk-in) | — |
| **Rack focus** | Limited | SH014 discovery (blue→white) | No rack as scare |
| **Absolute lock required** | Critical | SH020 (thread moves — camera must not) | Any jitter invalidates witness credibility |
