# SH030 — Shot Specification (Golden Shot)

**ID:** SH030  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Timecode:** 01:27–01:30  
**Act:** IV — Supernatural Attribution Crisis  
**Time Period:** 2026  
**Required Content:** Reader Man questions attribution to Desmond  
**Genre State Before:** Mark says father is dead; voice responds (SH029) — attribution crisis peaked  
**Genre State After:** No answer given — crisis deepens; Reader Man's ignorance is structural

---

## Narrative Intention

This shot enforces the trailer's central restraint: **the film knows it does not know**. Reader Man, the community's candidate for knowing, explicitly questions — not confirms — that the presence is Desmond. He does so *without authority*.

SH030 must accomplish two contradictory things: raise the supernatural question ("Is this Desmond?") and refuse to answer it. The audience leaves this shot *more* uncertain than they entered.

**Primary Question Planted:** *If even the Reader Man won't claim it is Desmond, who — or what — is it?*

---

## Audience Knowledge

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| They heard SH029: Mark says "Mi father dead" (patwah) — and a voice answers from beyond the door. The natural reading is Desmond's duppy. | The film's own spiritual investigator declines that reading. He suggests alternatives (unspoken) or simply holds ignorance. The audience now holds *competing hypotheses* without adjudication. |

## Scene Context

- **Location:** Family house, bedroom corridor — door that haunted SH024 (John hears voice) and SH028 (footsteps). Now Reader Man stands before it.
- **Participants:** Reader Man (foreground/inquiry), women (Ione, Yvonne, Marcia — holding shirt: SH026 formation, now observing), Mark/John off-frame or in doorway.
- **Dramatic Beat:** Interrogation of evidence — not person. Reader Man examines shirt or door; asks the question about attribution.
- **Time:** Minutes after SH029 — continuous.

## Camera Setup

| Parameter | Specification | Rationale |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Camera** | A-cam LF on sticks; B-cam handheld for coverage (PROPOSED) | Reader Man's authority is formal, not frantic — sticks for primary; handheld could mark community's unease but must not overpower |
| **Framing** | Medium two-shot: Reader Man foreground (camera-right, 3/4 profile), door deep center, women soft midground camera-left. 2.39:1: verticality emphasized — Reader Man standing, door tall. | Composition encodes question: Reader Man faces threshold, not camera; women witness between investigator and mystery |
| **Angle** | Eye level, straight-on — slightly low if Reader Man is tall (PROPOSED: 10° low) to give him gravity without granting supernatural power | Avoids Dutch (trick) and high (diminishing) — neutral witness |
| **Movement** | Static — slight drift (0.5″ over 3s) acceptable if motivated as handheld witness; but preferably locked | Stillness preserves interrogation clarity |
| **Depth of Field** | Hold Reader Man + door + women acceptable focus — T4; deep enough for geography | Geography matters: shirt, man, door must be simultaneously legible |

**Alternative Witness Note:** Should Reader Man be framed alone (isolated authority) vs with women (witnessed inquiry)? **With women** is preferred — it makes his not-knowing a social event, not solo declaration.

## Lens and Framing

- **Lens (PROPOSED):** 35mm Cooke Panchro Classic on LF — natural perspective for corridor; includes door height without distortion.
- **Aperture:** T4.0 — hold geography.
- **Focus:** On Reader Man's face/eyes (sharp), door slightly soft (holds mystery), women acceptable.

## Blocking

| Element | Position | Movement |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Reader Man** | Camera-right foreground, 3/4 to door, holding shirt or gesturing toward stitching (not grasping — respecting women's ownership) | Turns slightly toward Mark/women as he questions; does not step toward door (he does not engage presence directly — he questions evidence) |
| **Door** | Deep center, closed, corridor dimly behind | Still — but in deep background, faint light or shadow could be argued as presence (PROPOSED: no visible figure — keep threshold empty; footsteps in SH028 are acoustic, not visual) |
| **Ione/Yvonne/Marcia** | Midground camera-left, holding shirt between them (continuation of SH026 composition) | Listening — Ione steady, Yvonne reactive, Marcia uncertain |
| **Mark/John** | Off-frame or doorway edge | Listening — Mark affected by SH029 dialogue |

**Eyelines:**
- Reader Man → Mark (when questioning attribution) or → shirt (when questioning stitching)
- Women → Reader Man (attention), then → door (fear)
- No one looks to camera.

---

## Lighting

- **Style:** 2026 interior — neutral daylight from off-frame window (camera-left, 45° soft) + corridor practical depth.
- **Key:** Soft window (5600K, diffused) camera-left — wraps Reader Man and women.
- **Fill:** White bounce corridor — ratio 2:1; reveals door while keeping corridor mysterious.
- **Door Separation:** Subtle edge (Aputure 120D with 30° grid, 15% level) from corridor practical motivation — separates Reader Man silhouette without theatricality.
- **Color:** Neutral — no stylized color coding; this is an inquiry, not a ritual.
- **Motivation:** All lighting motivated as window/corridor practical. No supernatural color.

## Performance

| Character | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Reader Man** | **Questions, does not declare.** Performance: restrained, thoughtful, slightly troubled — not theatrical Obeah caricature. He is a community figure who knows his limits. He looks at stitching: "No... dis not necessarily Desmond." (PROPOSED line — pending dialogue lock; but SH030's text is *questioning attribution*). He does not raise voice; he lowers it. He does NOT perform ritual gesture; he gestures toward shirt. His authority is *epistemological restraint*, not charisma. |
| **Women** | Ione: steady — she lived the original repair; she watches Reader Man's honesty. Yvonne: reactive — SH021's fear still in body. Marcia: uncertainty. No one interrupts. |
| **Mark** | Off-frame reaction — hearing that even Reader Man won't confirm father's presence deepens his SH029 confrontation. |

**Dialect/Culture Note:** Reader Man's patwah and register must be authentic Jamaican, not invented exotic. Cast Jamaican actor whose presence reads as lived community role. Avoid any "mystic" costuming tropes (no beads added for trailer effect).

## Editorial Transition

- **In:** Cut from SH029 (Mark: father is dead; voice responds). Reader Man's question is immediate counterpoint — cut hard, no dissolve.
