# SH007 — Shot Specification (Golden Shot)

**ID:** SH007  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Timecode:** 00:17–00:20  
**Act:** I→II — Tragicomedy → Domestic Mystery (transition point)  
**Time Period:** 2026 — Funeral  
**Required Content:** Congregation laughter conceals fourth voice  
**Genre State Before:** Warm communal laughter (SH004–006)  
**Genre State After:** "Something else is laughing" — audience notices or subconsciously registers excess

---

## Narrative Intention

This is the trailer's first **auditory clue that acquires new meaning later**. In SH007, laughter is communal: Yvonne, Ione, and Marcia laugh at Desmond's remembered line. But the sound field contains **one voice too many** — a fourth laugh that does not correspond to any visible laugher. At first viewing, the audience may not consciously count. On second viewing (or after SH031 isolates the fourth laugh), they realize the room was never empty.

The shot must accomplish in 3 seconds what the film accomplishes over 90 minutes: make the audience mistrust what they have already heard.

**Primary Question Planted:** *Did you hear that extra voice, or did you imagine it?*

---

## Audience Knowledge

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Laughter is warm, earned, communal. Yvonne, Ione, Marcia each laughed (SH004–006). Matthew smiled. The room is alive. | Laughter *contains* an anomaly. The audience now carries an unprocessed auditory excess. Their Genre state subtly shifts from comfort to inquiry — even if they cannot name why. The Witness reviewer must confirm this shift is subliminal, not overt. |

---

## Scene Context

- **Location:** Same church as SH001–006 (continuous).
- **Participants Visible:** Congregation (~40–60), Matthew at pulpit reacting, Matthew's brothers, Ione/Yvonne/Marcia (already established laughing).
- **Invisible Participant:** The fourth voice — its source unconfirmed. In-world possibilities: congregant off-screen, acoustic reflection, or uncanny presence. Trailer must not decide. The sound must be physically plausible as *both* a hidden congregant and something else.

## Camera Setup

| Parameter | Specification | Rationale |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Camera** | Alexa Mini LF, on sticks, medium-wide | Witness stance — we are not hunting the laugh; we observe the room that contains it |
| **Framing** | Church mid-wide — congregation in foreground/midground, pulpit soft in deep background. 2.39:1: left third congregation, right two-thirds aisle + pulpit out-of-focus. | Shows social space; audience counts (or fails to count) laughers visually while hearing too many |
| **Angle** | Eye level, straight-on (not high, not low) | Democratic — everyone in room shares frame equally |
| **Movement** | Subtle push-in (2% over 3s) or *completely static* — director's choice. | If push: subconscious tightening. If static: respects stillness doctrine. Either is valid; push is PROPOSED. |
| **Depth of Field** | Deep enough to hold 3–4 rows of congregation in acceptable focus; pulpit slightly soft | Forces audience to scan room |

**Alternative (Witness Question):** Should camera pan/search for the fourth voice? **No** — the camera must not know more than the mourners. It witnesses the room; it does not investigate.

---

## Lens and Framing

- **Lens (PROPOSED):** 32mm Cooke Panchro Classic on LF — wide enough for mid-wide room, minimal distortion of faces, human falloff.
- **Aperture (PROPOSED):** T2.8–T4.0 — holds congregation in focus, softens deep pulpit (which will sharpen in SH008's cut to Matthew).
- **Focus:** Manual. Focus on mid-congregation (Yvonne/Marcia row). Allow slight breathing if using push.

## Lighting

- **Style:** Church available light — warm, natural, windows camera-right providing soft key; overhead fluorescents or practicals filling.
- **Key:** Window daylight (5500K) softened through sheer — camera-right, 45°.
- **Fill:** Bounce from aisle, unbleached muslin — low ratio (1.5:1) to keep warmth.
- **Practicals:** Church overheads if attractive; otherwise turn off and rely on window + bounce + subtle LED panels hidden as congregation eye-light (very low level, PROPOSED).
- **Color:** Warm (tragicomedy act) — not yet cool. Continuity with SH004–006.

## Sound Design

This is the **most important sound design in the trailer** — it must work twice.

| Element | Design | Mix Notes |
|---------|--------|-----------|
| **Communal laughter** | 10–15 voices, centered + slightly spread (church acoustic, early reflections from stone/wood) | Stereo base + 5.1 surround; anchored visually |
| **Fourth voice** | Single voice, slightly off — timbre ambiguous (could be elderly woman, could be Desmond's throat clearing's tonal cousin, could be...). Slightly **younger or older** than expected — not clearly matched to any visible person. Placed **~10–15° off-axis** in surround, and with **slightly longer pre-delay** than visible laughers. | Level: just below conscious threshold — 3–4 dB below main laughter, masked. On solo (SH031 isolation), this same stem is lifted to 0 dB above room. Must be same file for verification T08. |
| **Room** | Church reverb — same impulse as SH001–006 | Ties shots |
| **Transition to SH008** | Laughter begins to thin; Matthew's line "Daddy used to love that story" will cut through | Laughter decays naturally; fourth voice may be last to fade (PROPOSED — director's choice) |

**Technical Requirement:** Fourth voice must be **separate stem** (track `SH007_FOURTH_VOICE`) bounced to isolation for SH031. Same performance file used in both shots — validator T08 checks file hash or reference.

**Provenance:** Record in same church. Record congregation laughter ensemble; record fourth voice individually in same space with same mic position but performer turned slightly off-axis (creates subtle spatial difference without unnatural processing).

## Practical Execution Method

1. Block congregation; mark Ione/Yvonne/Marcia positions.
2. Playback Matthew's SH001 line over speaker for extras to react to (captured for sync).
3. Record ensemble laughter: 5+ takes with whole room laughing naturally.
4. Record fourth voice wild: single performer (PROPOSED: cast elderly woman whose voice could also plausibly belong to someone in congregation but not clearly identified) — record in same position but rotated ~15° off ensemble orientation.
5. Record room tone.
6. Slate both audio stems with identical timecode for later sync verification.
