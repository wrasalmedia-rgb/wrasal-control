## Narrative Intention

This shot **reuses the same intended audio source** as SH007 to prove that the audience's earlier experience contained more than they noticed. In SH007, the fourth laugh was masked. In SH031, it is **isolated** — the communal laughter thins or drops, leaving the fourth voice alone or dominant. The image returns to the funeral (the origin scene: Matthew's eulogy, the congregation, perhaps Matthew's face). The audience now hears SH007's sound differently because they have seen SH020 (the red thread move) and heard SH029 (the voice answer). *Memory is re-edited*.

This recontextualization is the trailer's structural thesis: **supernatural understanding does not add new evidence; it reframes what was already there**.

**Primary Question Planted:** *How many times did you miss it?*
