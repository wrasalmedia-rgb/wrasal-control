# SH014 — Shot Specification (Golden Shot)

**ID:** SH014  
**Duration:** 3s  
**Timecode:** 00:39–00:42  
**Act:** II — Domestic Mystery  
**Time Period:** 1980s  
**Required Content:** White repair discovered over blue stitching  
**Genre State Before:** Ione's craft and care (SH013 blue stitching)  
**Genre State After:** Suspicion — domestic evidence does not match domestic story

---

## Narrative Intention

This is the **material evidence shot** — the trailer's central clue imaged as physical fact. Ione completed a blue-thread repair (SH013). Now she discovers — on the same hem, in the same shirt — a **white repair over her blue stitching**. The camera must show this without interpreting it. No character says "infidelity." The *evidence says it*, and the *audience* feels Ione's suspicion before she voices it.

The shot separates **observation from inference**: what we see (white over blue) vs. what it means (someone else sewed this). That separation is the film's epistemological method.

**Primary Question Planted:** *Who sewed this, and where was Desmond when it was done?*

---

## Audience Knowledge

| Before | After |
|--------|-------|
| Ione repaired Desmond's shirt with blue thread — an act of care (SH013). We trust her craft. | The same hem has been worked over by another hand. The shirt carries a second biography. The audience now suspects Desmond's movements away from home, but has *no confirmation* — only thread. |

---

## Scene Context

- **Location:** Ione and Desmond's home, 1980s — bedroom or laundry area (warm, domestic).
- **Time:** Weeks or months after SH013 (same shirt, same hem, returned differently).
- **Action:** Ione holds the shirt to fold/repair/inspect. Her thumb encounters resistance where she expects blue. She turns the hem.
- **Dramatic Beat:** Discovery — quiet, not explosive. The mystery is domestic.

---

## Camera Setup

| Parameter | Specification | Rationale |
|-----------|---------------|-----------|
| **Camera** | LF on sticks with macro capability or diopter | Macro witnesses material truth; no camera trick simulates thread |
| **Framing** | Extreme close-up (ECU) — hem and fingers only. 2.39:1: hem runs horizontally across lower third; fingers enter frame. Shirt texture fills background bokeh. | Forces audience to read thread as evidence; mirrors Ione's attention |
| **Angle** | Overhead/45° — looking down at fabric in hands, as Ione sees it | Subjective proximity without pretending camera is her eye |
| **Movement** | Micro-adjustment — camera follows finger trace along hem (1–2 inch move). No dramatic push. | Witnesses discovery; does not add suspense artificially |
| **Focus** | Manual rack: blue thread in focus → white thread snaps into focus as hem turned | Focus shift *is* discovery |

**Alternative Witness Proposal:** Add B-camera medium of Ione's face seeing this (used in SH015). SH014 itself stays on evidence — face is SH015's job.

---

## Lens and Framing

- **Lens (PROPOSED):** 60mm or 100mm macro (Cooke or Leica R macro) on LF — true macro for stitch detail, not crop. At ~1:1 or 1:2 magnification, stitch geometry is readable.
- **Aperture (PROPOSED):** T4.0–T5.6 — enough depth to hold two thread layers in focus after rack, but shallow enough to isolate hem from shirt mass.
- **Extension/Diopter:** If 100mm not true macro, add 20mm extension tube; test stitch visibility.
- **Resolution:** 4K+ to resolve thread texture (cotton fuzz vs polyester smoothness difference is part of evidence).

---

## Blocking

| Element | Position | Movement |
|---------|----------|----------|
| **Ione's hands** | Center frame, holding white shirt hem between thumbs/forefingers | Initial hold (0.5s) → thumb traces blue stitches → encounters white overlap → fingers turn hem to reveal white over blue (0.9s) → hold for audience read (0.9s) |
| **Blue thread** | Visible as lower row — slightly irregular, duller | Under white thread |
| **White thread** | Overlapping — tighter, brighter, straighter tension | Over blue thread — physically plausible as repair over repair |
| **Shirt body** | Out-of-focus mass above hem | Provides warm white context |

**Hands Performance:** Ione's hands are a character. Actress's hands (or hand double if necessary) must convey: stillness → tactile search → micro-pause when white encountered → deliberate turn to confirm. No trembling (that's SH021's Yvonne); this is *inquiry*, not fear.

---

## Lighting

- **Style:** 1980s domestic interior — window daylight, warm, practical.
- **Key (PROPOSED):** Soft window daylight (5600K, diffused) camera-left 30° — rakes across hem at low angle to reveal thread texture through shadow.
- **Fill:** Bounce from domestic surface (white wall/yellowed sheet) — warm fill.
- **Texture Light:** Small hard accent (300W fresnel or Aputure 120D with snoot) camera-right 10°, very low level — specifically to rake thread texture. This light is **motivated** as gap in curtain or desk lamp spill.
- **Color:** Warm 1980s domestic — slightly yellowed whites (fabric not blue-white; period-correct). Blue thread vs white thread color difference must remain readable even in warm light — test grade.

**Witness Review:** Lighting must not make white thread dramatically "glow" (that is SH019's red thread role). It is simply *whiter*.

## Editorial Transition

- **In:** Cut from SH013 (blue stitching ECU). Match cut on hem action — continuity of hands/fabric but thread color change registers as time/ownership jump.
- **Out:** Cut to SH015 (Ione questions repair) — face shot; evidence to inference.
- **Hold:** 3s is generous — allows audience to read evidence. Do not shorten. Witness validates: average viewer needs ~1.2s to identify second thread layer.

## Material Requirements

- Single hero shirt prop with **both blue and white repairs on same hem** (built sequentially; blue applied first in correct geometry, white added over).
- Backup shirts for multiple takes (3 hero shirts minimum).
- Hand model continuity (Ione actress or approved hand double).
- Macro lens + stable sticks + monitor for stitch verification on set.
- 1980s domestic set (bed, ironing board, or laundry table).
- Ione's 1980s wardrobe already established.

---

## Practical Execution Method

1. Prop master builds hero shirt: white cotton poplin; hem stitched with dull blue cotton (hand-stitched irregular); then white polyester over blue (tighter, brighter) on same 12cm hem section.
2. Test visibility: macro photograph hem under proposed lighting; confirm blue vs white legibility at 4K on scope monitor.
3. On set: Ione actress rehearses hand discovery without dialogue; director times finger turn to 0.9s discovery window.
4. Shoot macro ECU: manual focus rack blue→white; 8+ takes for finger performance.
5. Shoot companion medium (for SH015 coverage) — face reaction, same moment.
6. Record fabric foley separately with close mic for mix control.

---

## Optional VFX Requirements

None. All practical. VFX must not be used to create thread — material continuity requires practical prop truth.
