## Narrative Intention

To invoke the film's governing questions — memory, fidelity, voice — before any image appears. The audience enters as mourners: they see nothing, they hear a man's voice carry a joke that is simultaneously vulgar, tender, and communal. The black screen makes the *audience's own imagination* the first visual. By the time the image arrives (SH002), the joke has already divided — and bound — the room.

Matthew's line is not provocation; it is elegy. It memorializes Desmond by repeating his story, and it tests whether the community's laughter can survive the father's death.

**Primary Question Planted:** *Who is laughing, and why?*

## Performance

| Character | Direction |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Matthew** (early 40s, Matthew-born ~1982) | Delivers line with **affection, not crudeness**. This is how his father told it — with joy, not malice.
