## 1. Logline & Genre Engine

### Logline
At Desmond's funeral, Matthew's eulogy joke triggers a laughter that doesn't belong. Between 1980s Kingston and present-day Jamaica, a white shirt's impossible red stitching forces three sisters and four brothers — bound by one father and three mothers — to confront whether their father's legacy is memory, mystery, or duppy.

### Genre Progression (Doctrine-Driven)

> **THE GENRE CHANGES WHEN THE AUDIENCE'S UNDERSTANDING CHANGES.**

The trailer is engineered to shift genre *in the audience's mind*, not merely in production design:

| Act | Timecode | Genre State | Audience Understanding | Cue For Editor |
|-----|----------|-------------|------------------------|----------------|
| **I — Elegy** | 00:00–00:17 | Jamaican family tragicomedy | "We are at a warm, funny Jamaican funeral" | Laughter is communal |
| **II — Inquiry** | 00:17–00:44 | Domestic mystery | "Something about the shirt doesn't add up" | Ione's suspicion; white-over-blue |
| **III — Uncanny** | 00:44–01:08 | Jamaican Gothic horror | "The house is listening" | Fourth laugh isolated; voices behind doors; red thread moves |
| **IV — Attribution Crisis** | 01:08–01:45 | Supernatural attribution crisis | "Who — or what — is doing this?" — no answer given | Reader Man questions, does not answer |

**Doctrinal Application:**
- **Reality First. Technology Second.** — Every effect must have a physically plausible photographic base before any VFX augmentation. The camera is a witness.
- **Camera as Witness, Not Magician.** — No impossible camera moves that editorially conjure scares; fear comes from what the witness sees, not camera trickery.
- **Genre Changes When Understanding Changes.** — The same laughter (SH007→SH031) means something different after the audience has seen SH020.

## 3. The White Shirt — Narrative and Material Logic

### 3.1 Narrative Role

The white shirt is **the film's only physical evidence** of the supernatural. It is:
- A garment (Desmond's, 1980s)
- A clue (white over blue)
- An impossible object (red thread that moves)

### 3.2 Material State Graph (Summary)

```
[Garment: White cotton dress shirt, Desmond, c. 1984]
        │
        ▼ SH013
[Ione repairs hem — Blue cotton thread, hand-stitched, slightly irregular]
        │
        ▼ SH014 (weeks/months later, same hem)
[White polyester thread over blue — neater, whiter, different tension]
        │  ← Ione's discovery triggers suspicion (SH014–017)
        │  ← Desmond's vague explanation (SH016) — no confirmation of infidelity
        │
        ▼ Decades of storage (aging, yellowing, fold lines)
        │  SH018: Yvonne handles shirt in 2026
        ▼ SH019
[Red thread revealed beneath older repairs — vivid, waxed, blood-like]
        │
        ▼ SH020
[Red thread retracts impossibly — no hand, no pull, ~1.5s movement]
        │
        ▼ SH021–030
[Red stitching questioned by Reader Man — attribution crisis]
```

**Physical Construction Constraints (for Prop Master):**
- Base fabric: White cotton poplin, period-appropriate (no modern poly-cotton sheen)
- Blue thread: Dull cotton, visible hand irregularity (Ione's character detail)
- White thread: Brighter, tighter tension, different stitch geometry
- Red thread: Waxed, slightly thicker, impossible to source in 1980s Jamaican haberdashery — this is intentional uncanny detail
- Aging: Fabric yellows at collar/cuffs; thread retains color; fold lines visible in 2026

Full graph with stitch geometry, visibility per shot, and construction method in: `04-continuity/white-shirt-material-graph.md`

| SH031 | Fourth laugh isolated (recontextualized SH007 audio) | "It was there from the beginning" | Editorial recontextualization |

### 4.2 What the Trailer Must Not Claim

- ❌ That the presence IS Desmond as duppy (may be, but not confirmed)
- ❌ That red thread is a traditional Jamaican spiritual mechanism (it is FICTIONAL storyworld invention)
- ❌ That Reader Man can identify the presence (he questions, does not declare)
- ❌ That women's reconciliation or moral resolution occurs (unresolved)

### 4.3 Reader Man Doctrine

Reader Man is **a proposed spiritual investigator whose exact methods and biography remain undefined**. He:
- Enters the family house (SH025) — not summoned with ritual authority
- Examines the shirt with the women (SH026)
- Questions the red stitching (SH027, SH030) — he *questions attribution to Desmond* (SH030)

His ignorance is structural: if he knew, the attribution crisis would collapse.
