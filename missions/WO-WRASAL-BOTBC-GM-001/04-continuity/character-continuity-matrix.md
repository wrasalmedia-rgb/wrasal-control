## Evaluation Dimensions (Independent)

Per Mission Directive §10:

| Dim | Name | What It Measures | Failure Mode |
|-----|------|------------------|--------------|
| D1 | **Identity Fidelity** | Recognizably same person across 1980s/2026 | Audience cannot track who is who |
| D2 | **Biological Realism** | Age/plausibility (Ione 20s→60s, etc.) | Aging looks fake or impossible |
| D3 | **Physical Plausibility** | Bodies, gait, proportions consistent | Body changes break realism |
| D4 | **Styling Control** | Hair/makeup/wardrobe per period | Anachronistic styling |
| D5 | **Cinematic Coherence** | Performance + framing = coherent character | Character reads as inconsistent |
| D6 | **Temporal Continuity** | No anachronism between periods | Modern styling in 1980s |

Each dimension is assessed independently. A character may pass D1/D5 while failing D4 — this granularity is required to distinguish costume failure from casting failure.
