## 1. Fundamental Validation Principles

1. **Humanity Before Technology (`INV-00`):** *No human being shall be reduced to the digital representation created from them.* Upstream `HUMAN WELFARE` checks (`@AC-00`, `@AC-13`, `@AC-15`) execute **before** identity ingestion or technical quality evaluation.
2. **Visual Resemblance ≠ Authorization (`@AC-09`):** Technical image quality (`Yute::ValidationService`) and sovereign human consent/approval (`Yute::ConsentAuthority` + `Yute::ApprovalLedger`) are orthogonal gates. Identity is certified **only** by explicit human attestation anchored to verified provenance.
3. **Light Means Clarity (`@AC-14`):** Every record and export must distinguish real vs. synthetic, authorized vs. unlicensed, remembered vs. reconstructed, verified vs. claimed, and welfare-serving vs. exploitative.
