## Evidence chain and admission checks

For a candidate payment, preserve and evaluate evidence in this order without collapsing the stages:

1. Obligation/assessment exists and is attributable to the user (if any).
2. Payment was intended and authorized (instruction/approval).
3. Funds were debited or tendered (sender bank/processor record).
4. Transfer provider accepted, processed, paid out, returned, cancelled, or left it pending (provider trace).
5. Intended recipient received funds (recipient-side proof).
6. Creditor posted and allocated the receipt to the correct obligation/account.
7. Any remaining balance is supported by creditor records and reconciled arithmetic.

Each stage needs its own source and evidence ID. Hash original bytes before extraction; hash is integrity only. Record custody/provenance, apparent-authenticity observations, and independent issuer verification separately. If evidence is missing, conflicting, or incomplete, record an exception and leave the outcome unresolved.

## Official public-source notes (checked 2026-10-25)

- [TAJ eServices](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/eservices?inheritRedirect=true) describes filings, payments, payment receipts and other taxpayer services. It is not access to the user’s private account.
- [TAJ Tax Portal FAQs](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/tax-portal-faqs) state an individual can register to check returns/payments even when an agent files. The [TAJ terms](https://www.jamaicatax.gov.jm/web/guest/terms-of-use) caution that ordinary email may not be secure for confidential data and refer to identity/authentication for personal account information.
- SLB’s [loan-statement glossary](https://www.slbja.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/SLB-Loan-Statement-Glossary.pdf) defines, among other fields, opening principal, principal paid, interest, fees, total payment, net payments and payment reversals; it is a useful completeness check, not an account statement.
- SLB’s [official press release](https://www.slbja.com/debt-reset-press-release/) reports Debt Reset extended to December 2026. The live [Debt Reset FAQ](https://www.slbja.com/debt-reset/) has conflicting timing text (one answer refers to a December–April payment window; another says the programme runs through December 31, 2026). Confirm the specific borrower/loan terms and deadline in writing before relying on a benefit.
- BOJ’s current [remittance list](https://boj.org.jm/core-functions/financial-system/list-of-remittance-companies/) was shown as 225 companies/466 locations as at October 16, 2026. This time-bound list must be checked against the specific agent/location/date; it cannot prove an individual transfer.
- BOJ’s [credit reporting page](https://boj.org.jm/core-functions/financial-system/credit-reporting/) lists licensed bureau routes; its [FAQ](https://boj.org.jm/about-boj/faq/) describes consumer access to their own credit information, sources, prior disclosures and a free annual report. A credit report remains supporting evidence, not a complete ledger.
