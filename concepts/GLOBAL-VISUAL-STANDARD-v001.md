This document transcribes the "GLOBAL VISUAL STANDARD v001" reference sheet as a non-runtime
concept record for the WRASAL portfolio. It is an agnostic prompt library / cinematic reference:
production-quality, copy-ready prompts for Midjourney 6.1, Flux Pro, and Sora that preserve the
standard's lighting, material, and composition anchors. Replace `[SUBJECT]` with a talent brief

prototype, build, test, or deployment. Canonical acceptance remains pending.

### 01 HERO HALF-PORTRAIT — Top Center / Anchor Portrait

Purpose: The primary human anchor. Centered, confrontational, shirtless with black knit beanie.
Defines skin texture, gaze intensity, and the signature orange prism flare across chest.

Midjourney:

```
centered half-body portrait of [SUBJECT], shirtless athletic man wearing black knit beanie, intense direct gaze, dark background, directional light with warm orange fiery prism refractions and lens flares across chest, deep shadows, raw skin texture, cinematic grain, powerful authentic --ar 4:5 --style raw --chaos 5 --v 6.1
```

Flux Pro:

```
Photorealistic centered half-body portrait of an athletic man, shirtless, wearing a black knit beanie and thin chain, looking at camera with intense neutral expression, dark blue-black background, high contrast directional lighting with warm orange fire-like lens flares refracting across his chest, deep shadows, raw tactile skin texture, no gloss, cinematic, 85mm lens, shallow depth of field, shot on ARRI
```

Sora / video:

```
Static close-up portrait shot, an athletic man shirtless with black beanie staring at camera, subtle camera push-in 5% over 8 seconds, warm orange light leaks and prism flares slowly moving across his chest, dark moody background, atmospheric haze, intense, cinematic lighting, no dialogue, subtle fire crackle ambience, 8 seconds, 24fps
```

### 02 FULL BODY VOLCANIC — Top Right / Environment Scale

Purpose: Full figure to establish cloth behavior, ground texture, and scale against the spires.
Draped deep red fabric must read heavy, not silky.

Midjourney:

```
full body of [SUBJECT] shirtless athletic man wearing draped dark red cloth around waist, pendant necklace, barefoot on black cracked volcanic rock with sparse dark red grass, towering sharp black basalt spires with faint red glow cracks, dramatic overcast storm sky, centered symmetrical composition, strong verticals --ar 4:5 --style raw --chaos 5 --v 6.1
```

Flux Pro:

```
Full body photorealistic of a shirtless athletic man wearing a draped deep red cloth around waist and pendant necklace, barefoot on black cracked volcanic ground covered in dark red dry grass, background of towering sharp black monolith spires with glowing red cracks, dramatic overcast storm sky, centered, symmetrical, epic scale, desolate, 35mm lens, high detail terrain
```

Sora / video:

```
Wide locked-off shot, full body of athletic man standing barefoot on volcanic rock in red wasteland, wind moving red grass slightly, towering black spires in background under moving storm clouds, camera slow dolly in 10%, desolate epic mood, ambient wind howl, no music, 10 seconds, anamorphic
```

### 03 LIGHTING 1 — FACE IN RED — Chiaroscuro / Close-up

Purpose: Lighting test for red key. Half face falls to black, half is sculpted by saturated
directional red. Skin detail is critical: pores, sweat, no retouch.

Midjourney:

```
extreme close-up of [SUBJECT] athletic man's face, half face in deep shadow, half lit with intense directional red light from side, sweat, skin pores visible, serious stare, pure black background, chiaroscuro, raw texture, cinematic --ar 1:1 --style raw --chaos 5 --v 6.1
```

Flux Pro:

```
Photorealistic extreme macro close-up of an athletic man's face, half in deep shadow, half lit with intense directional saturated red light, sweat beads on skin, visible pores and stubble, serious neutral expression, pure black background, chiaroscuro lighting, 85mm macro lens, hyper-detailed skin texture, no beauty retouching
```

Sora / video:

```
Extreme close-up locked on face, half shadow half intense red key light, subject breathing slowly, sweat glistening, static camera with 2% micro push-in, pure black void background, tense, quiet, faint low-frequency hum, 8 seconds
```

### 04 LIGHTING 2 — TORCH ON CLIFF — Source Motivation / Ritual

Purpose: Establishes diegetic fire source. Figure on edge holding flame, illuminating face from
below. Background must fall to black, no sky.

Midjourney:

```
medium shot of [SUBJECT] shirtless athletic man holding a small flaming torch in dark, standing on edge of black cliff, face lit from below by fire glow, orange-red light flicker, deep black background, smoke haze, cinematic rim light, powerful silhouette --ar 3:4 --style raw --chaos 5 --v 6.1
```

Flux Pro:

```
Photorealistic medium shot of a shirtless athletic man holding a small handmade torch, standing on the edge of a black volcanic cliff at night, his face illuminated from below by warm flickering firelight, deep shadows, orange-red glow, subtle smoke haze, pure black background, cinematic, 50mm lens, dramatic low-key lighting
```

Sora / video:

```
Medium shot, man on cliff edge holding torch, flame flickering casting dancing orange light on his torso and face, wind, camera slow orbit left 15 degrees, dark abyss background, fire crackle and distant wind, handheld subtle shake, 10 seconds
```


Flux Pro:

```
Photorealistic back view of a shirtless athletic man with muscular back, head turned in profile, intense red backlight rim lighting the edge of his skin and creating glowing highlights on spine and shoulders, sweat sheen, deep shadows, dark volcanic environment, volumetric haze, 85mm lens, tactile skin texture
```

### 07 TEXTURE SWATCHES — 4 Materials / Macro System

Purpose: Material bible for compositing and product. All textures must feel dry, cracked, and
sun-baked, never wet gloss except skin sweat.

Midjourney:

```
grid of 4 material swatches: 1) black cracked volcanic basalt rock macro 2) dark blood-red dry grass on black soil 3) heavy draped dark red canvas cloth texture 4) close-up raw human skin with pores and sweat, arranged as flat lay, dark background, top light, tactile --ar 3:2 --style raw --chaos 3 --v 6.1
```

Flux Pro:

```
Photorealistic flat lay of 4 material swatches on black background: top left - macro black cracked volcanic basalt rock, top right - dark blood-red dry grass on black cracked soil, bottom left - heavy draped dark red canvas cloth with deep folds, bottom right - extreme close-up raw human skin texture with pores and sweat, even top lighting, tactile, no gloss except skin, 50mm macro
```

Sora / video:

```
Top-down locked shot of 4 material swatches on black surface, subtle camera drift, soft top light creating gentle shadows in fabric folds and rock cracks, no movement in materials, tactile focus pulls between textures, 8 seconds, macro detail
```
