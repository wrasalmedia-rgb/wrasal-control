## M02 V1.2 Verification (48546B a8f16faf)

- **Lesson §5B Child abuse — statutory:** People listed in SSL §413(1)(a) “immediately make a report or cause a report … when they have reasonable cause” + quote “No school … shall impose any conditions, including prior approval or prior notification” [M02-5]; then “After you report … you must immediately notify person in charge after report was made [M02-5]” + NYC ACS “simply telling supervisor is not equivalent … get Call I.D., ensure named.” Distinct from policy box: “Many agencies require all staff … that creates workplace duty, not law-based status … policy cannot be applied to delay statutory.” Verified distinct.
- **Lesson §5B Harm to self/others:** Separate legal basis, “Frontline staff generally do not make solo decisions … they immediately involve supervisor … who assesses imminence … least necessary [M02-4][M02-8].” Then bold limit: “**Important limit on ‘contact supervisor’: If danger is immediate, or supervisor … cannot be reached promptly, do not delay emergency response … ensure immediate safety first (stay if safe, call 911 … call/text 988 … follow agency emergency plan), then contact supervisor ASAP and document**.” Emergency and supervisor pathways distinct, non-gating.
- **Examples:** Ex5 Possible mandated report — statutory vs policy (same facts, two duties) — shows ensure filing via supervisor vs waiting. Other examples (1-4) do not conflate 911/SCR.
- **Assessment:** KC26 C teaches statutory immediate without prior approval then notify after + ensure filed vs policy handbook; KC27 B teaches ensure immediate safety + seek supervisor, with emergency exception “**If danger immediate or supervisor cannot be reached … do not delay … call 911/988 per agency emergency plan then contact supervisor ASAP**” and note “distinct from KC26 child-abuse … statutory must report immediately without prior approval.” Pathways distinct, cross-referenced.
- **Recap:** Distinct bullet for statutory reporting [M02-5] vs policy handbook vs imminent-risk supervisor assessment [M02-4][M02-8] with emergency exception “if danger immediate/supervisor unavailable, do not delay … 911/988 …” — 42 CFR Part 2 stricter noted separately.
