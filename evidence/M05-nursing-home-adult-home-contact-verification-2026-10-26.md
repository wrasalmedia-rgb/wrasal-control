**M05 manuscript text (not summary) already distinguishes settings — verify:**

- **§6 B Nursing home / adult care facility resident [M05-7]:** “Facility staff with mandated duties … typically to **NYS DOH Nursing Home Complaint Hotline 1-888-201-4563 (or via DOH centralized intake)** for nursing homes/adult homes; document and preserve evidence; ensure care need addressed immediately. If resident has qualifying disability in a Justice Center jurisdiction (e.g., certain ICFs, state-operated homes), also report to **Justice Center 1-855-373-2122** or via facility Justice Center protocol [M05-8]; Check facility — DOH vs Justice Center vs both depends on licensure.”
  Source [M05-7] Official NYSDOH: https://health.ny.gov/facilities/nursing/ ; https://health.ny.gov/facilities/adult_care/ — DOH centralizes nursing home and adult care facility complaints; 1-888-201-4563 is published as the DOH centralized complaint/Nursing Home Hotline. Adult homes (adult care facilities) also route via DOH central intake; the number is the same portal, with adult care triage to appropriate DOH unit. **Manuscript is correct that DOH is the entry point for both, but assessment of “both vs separate numbers” depends on intake routing — single published hotline, internal routing differs.**

- **§6 A Community-dwelling older adult — APS via local county DSS [M05-1][M05-4]:** County DSS APS unit; navigation via **NYSOFA/NY Connects 1-800-342-9871**, not DOH — distinct from facility route.

- **§6 C Mental-health/substance-use/developmental-disability program [M05-7][M05-8]:** **Justice Center where jurisdiction applies** (1-855-373-2122 https://justicecenter.ny.gov/), otherwise **OMH https://omh.ny.gov/ , OASAS https://oasas.ny.gov/ , OPWDD https://opwdd.ny.gov/** incident reporting — facility licensure determines mandatory reporter, not B.

- **§6 D Immediate danger/crime:** **911** medical/law-enforcement — does not replace APS/facility referral; APS does not replace 911 — see M05 §7.

- **§6 E Long-Term Care Ombudsman [M05-10]:** NYSOFA/NY Connects advocate, not enforcement intake — distinct pathway.

**Consent, capacity, protective interventions without universal rule — verified M05 keeps distinct, not universal:**

- **Community older adult with capacity [M05-5]:** APS voluntary, seeks consent; if adult with capacity refuses services, response limited — cannot compel like child protective; re-offer, safety plan, concrete supports.
- **Community older adult without/capacity question:** Still seek least-restrictive option; APS may explore guardianship referral via court only if lacks capacity and at risk — not staff decision [M05-1][M05-5].
- **Nursing home/adult home resident:** Resident rights + facility duty to ensure care/plan of correction; DOH survey, Justice Center where jurisdiction includes abuse/neglect of people with disabilities — protective interventions are regulatory/enforcement, not APS voluntary model.
- **Child (M04 cross-reference, not M05 rule):** SCR statutory without consent [M02/M04] — explicitly not applied to elders in M05 §5.
